## Thread

1/ Big cycle for Percolator ☕ We put the whole system through a full-delta security review before any upgrade went out — and it caught real issues while they were still on the workbench, not on the live network.

2/ Security: The review found a handful of ways a market could get stuck — including one where a tiny rounding leftover could freeze withdrawals for a whole group. We're fixing each at the source and holding the upgrade until they're closed. Caught early, by design.

3/ Correctness: We fixed a fee-accounting gap. Previously, if one side of a trade couldn't cover its full fee, the remainder was simply never collected. Now the shortfall is charged properly. Cleaner books, no quiet leakage.

4/ Proofs: Our automated proof suite — 327 checks that mathematically test the core engine against every possible input, not just sample cases — ran green, twice, on the latest code. That's the bar every release candidate has to clear. ✅

5/ Playground: The test environment was repointed onto six fresh, clean markets on the newest format, with docs and trading flows updated to match. Easier to try, fewer stale edge cases, a better first experience.

6/ Where things stand: the engine fixes are merged and reviewed, not yet live. They go to the network only after the remaining fixes land, the proof suite re-runs clean, and a human signs off. The playground refresh is already up.

7/ Steady, careful, shipping. Finding problems before users ever meet them is the whole job.
