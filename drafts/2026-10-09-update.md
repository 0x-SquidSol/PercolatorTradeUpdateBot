# Percolator update — 2026-10-09

> Draft X thread — review, edit, then post manually. Generated by dcc-watch.

## Thread

1/ First full cycle since devnet v2 opened to the public — and real traders put it through its paces. 🧪 They reported issues, we found the causes, and the fixes are already shipping.

2/ Reliability: a trade that was slow to confirm could tell you "nothing was sent" even though it had landed. Now every transaction we broadcast is followed all the way to its outcome, and a slow one is watched instead of silently giving up. No more phantom failures.

3/ Launches: new markets stopped getting their live price connected once an internal capacity limit quietly filled up — creators spent, then got refused. Fixed, and the app now checks your limit *before* you spend a thing. Launching also got ~0.15 SOL cheaper per market.

4/ Numbers you can trust: tiny profits no longer round to "$0.00", losses now show their minus sign, vault values no longer read $0 when a data feed hiccups, and split or partially-closed trades are now recorded in full in your history.

5/ Speed: our price service now batches its data reads — about 96% fewer calls — and we stopped the duplicate catch-up jobs that piled up after each deploy. Newly created markets also show up in roughly a minute instead of up to six. ⚡

6/ Where things stand: devnet is a public test network — real software, test money. These app fixes are live there now; some indexer work is merged and waiting on final checks. Nothing here is on a main network. Thanks to everyone reporting bugs — keep them coming.
