# Percolator update — 2026-09-30

> Draft X thread — review, edit, then post manually. Generated by dcc-watch.

## Thread

1/ Shipping week 🚀 A batch of security fixes, a tougher price-feed safety net, and a build system that now refuses to compile against the wrong engine. Biggest item: a payout path that could be triggered without a signature is now locked down.

2/ Security: for positions held in escrow, the final payout instruction never checked that anyone had actually signed it — so a stranger could have routed the payout into an account nobody can spend from. Both payout routes now require a signature. Funds stay with their owner.

3/ Security: in the insurance/backing vault, deposits made while a loss was still outstanding could mint shares at a marked-down price and then capture the rebound from existing depositors. Those deposits are now rejected until the loss is settled. Fair pricing for everyone.

4/ Security: when a market closed out, one type of unsettled obligation could be released early — quietly pushing its share of a loss onto the remaining holders on that side. It's now gated so it can only release when it's genuinely safe. 🔒

5/ Reliability: our price keeper had two gaps — small prices were being rounded to a coarse step, and a safety limit on how far a price could move could be sidestepped by many small steps. Both fixed, with the limit now measured across every published price in a rolling window.

6/ Hardening: the program can no longer be built against an unverified copy of the risk engine — the build fails closed unless it matches the exact reviewed version. Plus new tests lock in past fixes, and unreachable old code was deleted. Fewer ways for a mistake to slip in.

7/ Where things stand: the price-keeper fixes are on the branch the live keeper runs. The program-level fixes are merged and reviewed, and go out to the network after final checks and a deploy sign-off. Steady, careful, shipping.

8/ More detail: https://github.com/dcccrypto/percolator-prog/pull/509 · https://github.com/dcccrypto/percolator-stake/pull/295 · https://github.com/dcccrypto/percolator-oracle-keeper/pull/127
