# Percolator update — 2026-09-30

> Draft X thread — review, edit, then post manually. Generated by dcc-watch.

## Thread

1/ Another heads-down cycle for Percolator — and a big one for safety. 🔒 We found and closed a handful of ways value could have leaked or gotten stuck, and we made our build refuse to run unless it's using the exact reviewed code.

2/ Security: an insurance-vault deposit could be timed for the moment the vault was temporarily marked down, then profit when it was restored — at the expense of existing depositors. Deposits are now blocked while a shortfall is outstanding. No more free step-up.

3/ Security: on two payout paths, a wallet held in escrow could have its payout routed somewhere unspendable by an unsigned caller. Those paths now require a proper signature. We also restored a missing safety gate on fee-policy changes and tightened a stale-price check to the right asset.

4/ Reliability: when a market wound down, one special kind of obligation could be released early — paying that account in full and quietly pushing its share of losses onto everyone else on the same side. Now gated, with new end-to-end tests against the real program.

5/ The price keeper: small repeated price steps could creep past our safety bound over time. The bound is now a rolling check across every published price, so slow drift is caught like a sudden jump. We also fixed a rounding issue that was flattening small price moves.

6/ Build discipline: our build now fails unless the risk engine it compiles matches the exact reviewed version — no more "whatever happens to be on disk." Plus fixes to the data service so trade sizes, prices and Earn balances are read correctly.

7/ Where things stand: all of this is merged and reviewed, with tests pinning each fix. The live devnet set moves after final checks — a few items are explicitly flagged as needing a human go before deploy. Steady, deliberate shipping. 🧱
