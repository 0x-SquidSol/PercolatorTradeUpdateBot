# Percolator update — 2026-10-06

> Draft X thread — review, edit, then post manually. Generated by dcc-watch.

## Thread

1/ Big week: everything we shipped was the run-up to Devnet v2, which goes live tomorrow, 1 October. 🚀 The headline — a huge batch of safety and reliability work landed in the app, all of it tested before anyone touches it.

2/ Reliability: sometimes a trade would fail because a piece of background bookkeeping had gone stale. Now the app quietly detects that, repairs it, and sends your transaction in one go. Fewer dead-end errors, less "try again later."

3/ Safety: we added clear guardrails around trade size — the app shows the maximum you can do, trims an oversized order down to what actually fits, and flags when a side is paused. You find out before you sign, not after it fails.

4/ Trust: errors used to show up as raw codes. They now read as plain explanations, plus a maintenance banner when something is being worked on, and checks that stop you entering a position that's about to be settled. Less guesswork, more clarity.

5/ Build discipline: the new features ship behind switches that are off by default, so each piece only turns on when it's ready. We also added thousands of lines of automated tests — including checks that the app's math matches the on-chain math exactly. ✅

6/ Where things stand: this work is merged and reviewed, and it's what makes tomorrow's Devnet v2 launch possible. Devnet is our public test network — real software, test money. Nothing here is deployed to a main network.

7/ Steady, careful shipping — then launch. See you tomorrow.
