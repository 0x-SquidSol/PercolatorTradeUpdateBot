# Percolator update — 2026-10-06

> Draft X thread — review, edit, then post manually. Generated by dcc-watch.

## Thread

1/ First full cycle with real traders on the public devnet — and the feedback loop is working. 🚀 Dozens of fixes shipped this week, almost all of them things real usage surfaced.

2/ Reliability: the most common complaint was the app saying "nothing was sent" or "you rejected it" for a transaction that had actually gone through. We now re-check the chain before reporting failure, so a completed action is reported as completed.

3/ Security: on the trading app's connection layer, two identical submissions could be quietly collapsed into one. Now send and simulate requests are never merged or cached — each one goes through on its own. We also cleared the remaining high-severity dependency alerts.

4/ Safety: in the on-chain vault code we found a case where a payout was priced against two pools but paid from one, leaving a creator stuck. Fixed, plus a new guard so a stranger can't lock a two-pool vault. Nine new tests cover it.

5/ Accuracy: a position could show different profit numbers on different screens. There's now one shared calculation behind every surface, and the test faucet, charts, portfolio and Earn pages all got honest-state fixes instead of misleading placeholders. 📊

6/ Where things stand: the app and data-service fixes are live on the public test network (real software, test money). The on-chain vault fix is merged and reviewed, and rolls out after final checks. Nothing here is on a main network.

7/ Big credit to community contributors — many of these fixes started as reports and PRs from users. Keep them coming.
